# Level Link

Link two versions of a level together and swap between them instantly from the
pause menu (or the level-complete screen) — without ever leaving "the run".

Typical use: keep a **StartPos / practice copy** linked to the **rated version**
of the same level, and jump between them mid-session with one keypress.

## How to use

1. Open any level's info page (or your own created level's screen). Tap the
   **link** button and either enter an **online level ID** or pick one of
   **your local levels**.
2. Play either level. Open the **pause menu**, or finish the run to 100%, and
   press the **Switch to Linked Level** key (default `T`, rebindable in the
   mod's settings) or click the swap button.
3. You're dropped into the linked level. The link is two-way, so the same key
   brings you back.

## Notes

- Links are stored per level and are two-directional.
- "Match Position" is experimental and visual-only — do not use it for
  legitimate leaderboard runs. By default the linked level starts normally
  (from its StartPos, if it has one).
